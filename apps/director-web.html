How to read this. A recorded appointment establishes that someone held a role for a period. It does not establish share ownership, involvement throughout that period, or responsibility for what the company did. Dissolution is a legal company status, not a finding of misconduct. Holding many appointments is ordinary for accountants, formation agents and group directors.

Dates. Appointments recorded before 1992 carry only an upper bound — the register says a person was appointed before a date, not on one. Those are shown as “before” and are never treated as exact.

Thresholds. The groupings below use published, adjustable windows: 30 days for resignations, 120 days for incorporations, and a minimum of three companies in a group.
